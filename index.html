About us

FIRST® Tech Challenge Team 22641 NEXER is a robotics team from the Prince's Royal College. Our team, which consists of passionate students ranging from 6th to 9th grade, all working together to learn, create, grow and express FIRST®'s Gracious Professionalism®. Our main goal is to be kind. We believe kindness builds stronger teamwork and better learning. Through robotics, we aim not only to design and compete but to inspire others with kindness, respect, and support.
